NEON BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY — DOWNTOWN · SUNSET BEACH · CINDER PEAKS

Leo Vance came to Neon Bay with empty pockets and big plans. Sixty main missions stand between him and the top of the city — and two locked districts, a five-star police force, and everyone who wants him gone. His old friend Maya waits by the Neon Diner with a lead.

Drive, swim, fly, race, play pool and golf, run shops, steal, and survive. One man, one city, no backup.
